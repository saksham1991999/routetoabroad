Guangzhou (Canton) has traded with the world since the Silk Road sea routes, and it shows: this is the pragmatic, food-obsessed, deal-making south. Travellers find it less polished than Shanghai and more fun for it. Business visitors find the point of the entire India–China trade corridor within a metro ride of our office.

## Trade and sourcing

Guangzhou sits in the Pearl River Delta, which is the world's densest manufacturing cluster. Shenzhen electronics, Foshan furniture and Dongguan factories are all within one to two hours. For India-bound buyers, the city is where sourcing, audits and freight planning meet.

### The Canton Fair

The Canton Fair is the world's largest trade show. It runs in three phases each April/May and October/November. Phase 1 covers electronics and machinery. Phase 2 covers consumer goods and home décor. Phase 3 covers textiles, apparel and medical products. Hotels triple in price during the fair, so book months ahead. Off-fair weeks suit factory visits better.

### Sourcing from Guangzhou


Do not pay 100% in advance. A common first-order structure is 30% deposit by TT and 70% against a copy of the Bill of Lading. Inspect at about 20% and 80% of production.

### Business visas

Trade fair visits and contract meetings need the M (business) visa, usually with an invitation letter from a Chinese company. A tourist L visa does not cover business. Check current rules before you book.

### Best time for a trade visit

October to December and March to April avoid the summer humidity. Align a visit with a Canton Fair phase only if you are buying.
