Beijing is where China presents itself to history and to the world. The city plan is deliberate — central axis, ring roads, imperial monuments embedded in a 21-million-person capital. For students it offers the country's two most famous universities; for travellers it holds the sites that put China on the imagination map; for business visitors it is the policy room where decisions start.
