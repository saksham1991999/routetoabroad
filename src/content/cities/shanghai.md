Shanghai has no Great Wall and no imperial palace — its theatre is commerce. The skyline on the Huangpu changes by the year, the metro is one of the world's largest and cleanest, and the city treats international residents as routine rather than spectacle. Students come for Fudan, SJTU and the internship economy; travellers for the skyline, the food and the art deco streets.
