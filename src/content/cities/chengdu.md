Chengdu sits in the fertile Sichuan Basin and has a reputation across China for knowing how to live well. Behind the teahouses and hotpot alleys, it is also a serious aerospace, electronics, and medical-research centre, with Tianfu International Airport serving direct regional flights across Asia and the Middle East.
