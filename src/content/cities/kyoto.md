Kyoto is compact enough to cross in 30 minutes yet dense enough to reward a week. Because peak-season crowds can overwhelm popular streets, timing and neighbourhood order matter more in Kyoto than anywhere else in Japan. We sequence itineraries by compass quadrant and early-morning slots so the city feels calm rather than congested.
