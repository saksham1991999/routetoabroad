Marco Polo called Hangzhou "the most beautiful and magnificent city in the world," and while the competition has caught up, West Lake still delivers the scene: mist, causeways, willows, pagodas. Students choose it for Zhejiang University's prestige at a discount; travellers for two days of scenery between Shanghai stops; founders for the Alibaba orbit.
