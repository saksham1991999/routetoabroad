**Short answer: fly into Tokyo, out of Osaka (or reverse), anchor on Kyoto 4 nights + Tokyo 3–4 nights, buy Shinkansen tickets individually, and budget US$120–200/day mid-range. Ten days, done properly.**

## The golden route (8–10 days)

**Days 1–4 · Tokyo** — Asakusa temples, Shibuya crossing, teamLab, Tsukiji outer market, day trip options (Nikko, Kamakura).
**Days 5–7 · Kyoto** — Fushimi Inari at dawn, Kinkaku-ji, Arashiyama bamboo grove, Gion in the evening; Nara's deer and Todai-ji as a half-day trip.
**Days 8–10 · Osaka (fly out)** — Dotonbori street food, Osaka Castle, or swap a day for Nara or Himeji Castle.

## Budget reality (per person, mid-range)

| Item | Daily (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| Business hotel / ryokan-lite | 60 – 120 |
| Food (konbini to izakaya) | 25 – 50 |
| Metro + local trains | 6 – 12 |
| Entries, temples, experiences | 10 – 25 |

Shinkansen Tokyo→Kyoto ≈ ¥13,000–14,000 (~US$90) one way — compare against the ¥50,000 nationwide JR Pass before buying. For Tokyo+Kyoto only, point tickets win.

## When to go

- **Sakura:** late March–mid April (book 5+ months ahead)
- **Momiji (autumn leaves):** mid November–early December in Kyoto
- **Avoid:** Golden Week (late April–early May) and Obon (mid August)
- **Winter:** cheap, quiet, uncrowded temples in light snow — underrated

## Visa and logistics

Indians need a Japan visa in advance (tourist, single/multi-entry) — documents are straightforward: form, photo, itinerary, financials, employment proof. Processing runs about 5–7 working days through VFS. We prepare and file both China and Japan visas together for dual-country trips, including cross-border flights.
