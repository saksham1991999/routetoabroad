**Short answer: HSK is China's official Mandarin proficiency exam. HSK 3 handles daily life, HSK 4 unlocks MBBS clinical rotations, and HSK 5 is the gate for Chinese-taught degrees. Start in your first semester — language is the most common self-inflicted delay.**

## What the levels mean

| Level | Words | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HSK 1 | ~150 | Greetings, numbers, basic survival phrases |
| HSK 2 | ~300 | Simple routines, shopping, directions |
| HSK 3 | ~600 | Campus life, casual conversation, short texts |
| HSK 4 | ~1,200 | Lectures on familiar topics, ward small-talk, news headlines |
| HSK 5 | ~2,500 | Academic Chinese-taught coursework, films, newspapers |
| HSK 6 | ~5,000+ | Professional fluency, research-level reading |

China introduced a revised standard ("HSK 3.0") that expands the band structure and vocabulary above the classic six levels. Paper-based testing worldwide still runs largely on the familiar 1–6 bands — check which standard your target exam centre applies.

## Timelines that actually work

- **HSK 2–3 (survival Chinese):** 3–6 months at 5 hrs/week
- **HSK 4 (MBBS clinical threshold):** 12–18 months from zero, or ~6 months from HSK 3
- **HSK 5 (Chinese-taught admission):** 2–3 years from zero

The pattern that works: 30–45 minutes daily beats 6 hours every Sunday. Character flashcards (Anki/Skritter), one textbook track, one listening habit (podcast or drama), and a weekly conversation hour.

## Exam mechanics

HSK runs paper-based and internet-based at test centres worldwide several times a year (registration closes about 3 weeks ahead). Listening, reading, writing — HSK 4 adds paragraph writing; HSK 5–6 add composition. There is also HSKK, the separate speaking exam, required by some Chinese-taught programmes.

## How we run HSK support

Placement test first, then a level-mapped study plan with exam dates baked into your programme timeline. For MBBS students, HSK 4 is scheduled before clinical years begin — not during them. Group classes in major student cities and online cohorts for everyone else.
