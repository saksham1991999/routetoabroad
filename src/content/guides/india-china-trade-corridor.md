**Short answer: the corridor is bigger and more functional than the headlines suggest — $100B+ a year of mostly routine commerce. What changed since 2020 is scrutiny, not prohibition. Importers who run tight documentation and inspection pipelines move goods smoothly; everyone else funds the demurrage economy.**

## What actually flows

**India imports from China:** electronics and components, machinery, telecom equipment, chemicals and pharma intermediates, solar cells, automotive parts, consumer goods, packaging.

**India exports to China:** iron ore and minerals, marine products, spices, chemicals, cotton, granite. The relationship is structurally asymmetric — India runs a large deficit — which shapes both governments' behaviour at the border and at customs.

## The compliance reality

- **FDI rules (2020–):** entities from land-border countries need government approval for investment in India — this constrains Chinese *investment*, not product imports.
- **Customs scrutiny:** electronics and machinery face deeper examination; valuation queries are common.
- **Product-level bans/approvals:** specific apps and products have been restricted; BIS/WPC registrations gate electronics and wireless goods.
- **Quality Control Orders (QCOs):** expanding across product categories — check whether your HS code carries a QCO before ordering.

## Ports and transit

| China port | India port | Sea transit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guangzhou / Shenzhen (Nansha, Yantian) | Mundra / JNPT (Nhava Sheva) | ~18–25 days |
| Shanghai / Ningbo | Mundra / JNPT | ~20–28 days |

Air freight (PVG/CAN → DEL/BOM): 3–7 days door-to-door at 5–8× sea cost. Watch peak-season roll risk (Canton Fair weeks, pre-Chinese New Year rush) and Golden Week port blank sailings.

## Payment rails

USD-settled by default. TT 30/70 for standard engagements; LC at sight for enterprise relationships; escrow for first deals. RMB settlement exists for large players but adds banking complexity on the Indian side. {{VERIFY}} current RBI/banking practice for your transaction size.

## Risk stack that holds up

1. Supplier audit before commitment
2. Bilingual spec + golden sample
3. 30/70 payments, escrow for firsts
4. AQL gates at 20%/80%
5. Pre-cleared HS/BIS/WPC paperwork
6. Buffer stock planning around known congestion windows

## 2026 outlook

Expect: continued import scrutiny on electronics, QCO expansion, faster rail+sea multimodal options via the Middle East, and rising Indian sourcing diversification ("China+1") that mostly routes through Vietnamese and Thai factories still fed by Chinese supply chains.
