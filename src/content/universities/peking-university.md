Peking University (Bei Da) anchors the Haidian academic district in northwest Beijing. For international students it offers the country's deepest bench of English-taught master's programmes in economics, policy and China studies, and research doctoral work across the sciences. Campus life centres on Weiming Lake and the surrounding imperial-era gardens.

**Who we place here:** high-performing applicants targeting master's/PhD tracks, CSC candidates with strong study plans, and language students aiming for the top HSK bands.

## What the programmes look like

Peking's programme list runs from Chinese language to research tracks. English-taught master's programmes include Economics & Management and International Relations. Computer Science & AI runs research tracks, and PhD programmes are CSC-funded and supervisor-led. Its MBBS is English-medium, MOE-listed and offered on limited seats.

Peking is ranked China #1–2 and top-15 worldwide (QS). Its state-key laboratories across disciplines offer CSC-funded research openings for master's and PhD applicants.

## Admissions requirements

Undergraduate entry is exam and interview based, with very small quotas. Master's and PhD entry is realistic for strong profiles. Requirements differ by level and programme, so confirm each point against the university's current notice.

- **Study plan:** for master's and PhD entry, a compelling study plan carries real weight.
- **Supervisor contact:** for research degrees, prior contact with a supervisor helps.

## Costs

A full CSC award covers tuition, dorm and most living costs. Use the [cost calculator](/tools/cost-calculator/) for a personal estimate.

## Intakes

Peking's main intake is September. Some programmes also take March entry. Confirm the programme list and each window with the university before you plan a file.

## Campus and location

Peking sits in Haidian, the academic district of northwest Beijing. Campus life centres on Weiming Lake. The Weiming Lake campus is a former imperial garden. Yan Yuan is arguably the most beautiful campus in China, and it sits minutes from Haidian's tech corridor.

## How to apply

1. Shortlist programmes against the admissions checks above.
2. Confirm the language level for each programme.
3. For CSC funding, pick Type A (through your home government) or Type B (direct to the university), and confirm current programme eligibility with Peking.
4. Prepare documents with the [document checklist](/tools/document-checklist/).
6. After an offer, the Admission Notice and JW202 start the [student visa](/guides/china-student-visa/) process.

## Realistic odds

No consultancy can guarantee admission or a CSC award. Committees decide. Apply to a mix of programmes, file a complete application, and treat this university as one target among several.

## Before you commit

- Confirm the exact programme and its language of instruction.
- Ask for the current fee schedule in writing.
- Ask when the JW202 is issued after acceptance.
- Keep a copy of every document you submit.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Name or date mismatches** between the Admission Notice and the JW202. Any mismatch means a re-issue ([student visa guide](/guides/china-student-visa/)).
- **Paying tuition to a personal account.** Pay only into the university's account.
- **Waiting too long for the JW202.** Slow issuance can disrupt the September intake ([student visa guide](/guides/china-student-visa/)).
- **Leaving China before the residence permit is issued.** The X1 stops working on exit.
