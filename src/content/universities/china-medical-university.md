Founded in 1931, China Medical University in Shenyang holds a storied place in Chinese medical education and was among the first institutions authorized by the Ministry of Education to admit international MBBS cohorts in English. Its modern Shenbei campus pairs purpose-built medical simulation facilities with some of Northeast China's busiest tertiary hospitals.

**Who we place here:** international medical applicants looking for a heritage MOE-listed university with a large, established international student community and lower northern living expenses.
