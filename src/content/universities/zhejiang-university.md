Zhejiang University's Zhijiang and Yuquan campuses thread through Hangzhou's green hills near West Lake. The university merged with Hangzhou University, Zhejiang Agricultural University and Zhejiang Medical University in 1998, making it one of China's largest comprehensive institutions — and one of its most productive at spinning out founders.

**Who we place here:** engineering and CS applicants who want elite rank without Beijing/Shanghai living costs, MBBS candidates, and scholarship-driven applicants — ZJU's CSC quota is generous by top-5 standards.
