Tsinghua sits across from Peking University in Haidian, and the rivalry is affectionate but real: Tsinghua owns engineering and technology; Peking owns humanities and pure sciences. The campus mixes early-20th-century American collegiate architecture (it began as a preparatory school funded by Boxer Indemnity returns) with striking modern labs.

**Who we place here:** engineering and CS applicants with strong marks and research intent, plus MBA-track professionals wanting China market immersion.

## What the programmes look like

Tsinghua's programme list centres on engineering and technology. It includes computer science and technology (research tracks), electrical engineering and automation, civil engineering, architecture and urban design, English-taught MBA and management science, and Chinese Language (IEP, Chinese-taught). Tsinghua is ranked China's #1 for engineering and computer science and sits in the global top-10 for several engineering fields (QS). Its English-taught flagship offerings, including the Schwarzman programmes, attract a globally competitive cohort.

## Admissions requirements

The engineering and computer science intake is small and intensely competitive. Match your profile honestly. Strong applicants often succeed through research-degree routes with supervisor backing.

- **Research degrees:** a prospective supervisor's support carries weight. Contact supervisors early.
- **Study plan:** at postgraduate level, a strong study plan matters as much as marks.

## Costs

A full CSC award covers tuition, dorm and most living costs. Use the [cost calculator](/tools/cost-calculator/) for a personal estimate.

## Intakes

September is Tsinghua's main intake. Confirm whether any programme takes another entry month, and confirm each application window with the university.

## How to apply

1. Shortlist programmes against the admissions checks above.
2. Confirm the language level for your programme.
3. For CSC funding, Tsinghua offers Type A and Type B routes. Read the [CSC guide](/guides/csc-scholarship/) to choose the route that fits your home country's process.
4. Prepare documents with the [document checklist](/tools/document-checklist/).
6. After an offer, the Admission Notice and JW202 start the [student visa](/guides/china-student-visa/) process.

## Realistic odds

No consultancy can guarantee admission or a CSC award. Committees decide. Apply to a mix of programmes, file a complete application, and treat this university as one target among several.

## Before you commit

- Confirm the exact programme and its language of instruction.
- Ask for the current fee schedule in writing.

- Keep a copy of every document you submit.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Name or date mismatches** between the Admission Notice and the JW202. Any mismatch means a re-issue ([student visa guide](/guides/china-student-visa/)).
- **Paying tuition to a personal account.** Pay only into the university's account.
- **Waiting too long for the JW202.** Slow issuance can disrupt the September intake ([student visa guide](/guides/china-student-visa/)).
- **Leaving China before the residence permit is issued.** The X1 stops working on exit.
