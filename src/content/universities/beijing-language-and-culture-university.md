Beijing Language and Culture University — affectionately known across Beijing as the "Little United Nations" — sits in the heart of Haidian's Wudaokou student district. Founded in 1962 under the Ministry of Education, it has trained more foreign diplomats, translators, business leaders, and sinologists in Mandarin than any other institution in China.

**Who we place here:** students worldwide seeking rapid HSK 1–6 mastery, a 1-year Mandarin foundation before a Chinese degree, or undergraduate/master's programmes in Business Chinese and Translation.
