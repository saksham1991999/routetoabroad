Fudan's Handan Road campus sits in Yangpu district, a student quarter of Shanghai that also houses Tongji University. The name means "heavenly light spreads over the earth" — confident, like the university. For international students, Fudan balances academic prestige with the practical advantage of Shanghai: everything from part-time work (which requires university permission and, for off-campus roles, additional immigration approval — your student residence permit has conditions) to airport connections is easier here.

**Who we place here:** business and economics applicants, MBBS candidates targeting Shanghai Medical College, and CSC applicants who want a comprehensive university in a global city.

## What the programmes look like

Fudan's programmes span business, economics, medicine and media. English-taught options include MBA/Finance, Economics (master's), International Business (bachelor's) and Public Health (master's). Shanghai Medical College anchors Fudan's MOE-listed, English-medium MBBS programme with top affiliated hospitals. The School of Economics and School of Management are among China's most respected. Journalism, international politics and Chinese studies draw international cohorts every September.

## Admissions requirements

Fudan suits business and economics applicants, MBBS candidates targeting Shanghai Medical College, and CSC applicants who want a comprehensive university in a global city. The MBBS programme is one of the most competitive for international seats.

- **MBBS:** HSK 4 is required before clinical rotations, per the [MBBS guide](/guides/mbbs-in-china/).
- **Study plan:** for postgraduate entry, the study plan matters as much as marks.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Name or date mismatches** between the Admission Notice and the JW202. Any mismatch means a re-issue ([student visa guide](/guides/china-student-visa/)).
- **Paying tuition to a personal account.** Pay only into the university's account.
- **Waiting too long for the JW202.** Slow issuance can disrupt the September intake ([student visa guide](/guides/china-student-visa/)).
- **Leaving China before the residence permit is issued.** The X1 stops working on exit.
