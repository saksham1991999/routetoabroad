Hebei University of Engineering, located in the historic city of Handan in southern Hebei Province, is a dedicated technical institution known for civil infrastructure, mechanical engineering, architecture, and water resources. Its new campus features modern engineering workshops and testing labs at a fraction of Beijing's living costs.

**Who we place here:** international engineering applicants focused on Civil, Mechanical, or Architectural Engineering who want MOE-recognized, practical technical training near Beijing on a modest budget.
