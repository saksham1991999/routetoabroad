The China Scholarship Council (CSC) scholarship is one of the most generous academic funding opportunities available to international students. Covering full tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend, and medical insurance, it can make an otherwise expensive degree completely accessible. Here is how to apply — from someone who did.

## Step 1: Determine your route — Type A vs Type B

There are two application routes. **Type A** runs through government channels: your country's Ministry of Education or designated authority nominates candidates. **Type B** is a direct application to a CSC-designated Chinese university — the route most applicants take because it is more accessible. You apply through the university's international admissions portal and the CSC system in parallel.

## Step 2: Choose your universities wisely

You can apply to up to three universities through the CSC portal. Strategize: one reach school, one target, one safety. Confirm each university actually accepts CSC applicants for your specific programme — not every programme at every university is CSC-eligible.

## Step 3: Prepare your documents

- Completed CSC online application form (campuschina.org / studyinchina.edu.cn portal)
- Passport valid for at least 12 months beyond the programme end
- Notarised academic transcripts and diploma certificates
- Study plan or research proposal (minimum 800 words)
- Two letters of recommendation (academic or professional)
- Physical examination form on the official CSC template
- Language proficiency proof (English medium certificate or HSK score)
- Police clearance certificate (for postgraduate applicants)

## Step 4: Submit before the deadline

CSC deadlines typically fall between late February and mid-April for the September intake. Where hard copies are required, courier them early — late submissions are disqualified without exception. Build a three-week buffer into your timeline.

## Step 5: Track and follow up

After submission, results roll in over the following 4–8 weeks. University admission decisions come separately. You need **both** the CSC award **and** the university's Admission Notice (with the JW202 form) before you can apply for your visa.
