China has emerged as one of the top destinations for Indian students pursuing MBBS degrees. With globally listed universities, English-taught programmes, and significant scholarship opportunities, the appeal is undeniable. But there are critical nuances every applicant should understand before committing.

## 1. Recognition is non-negotiable

Not every Chinese medical university is recognized by your home country's medical council. Always verify that your target university is listed in the WHO World Directory of Medical Schools and approved by regulators like the NMC (India), PMDC (Pakistan), or ECFMG (USA). RouteToAbroad only works with fully recognized, MOE-listed partner institutions.

## 2. Language is a two-phase journey

Most MBBS programmes are taught in English for the first years. Clinical rotations are different — you will be talking to patients in Chinese, which means clearing HSK 4 before you enter a ward. Start Mandarin classes in year one, not year three. Integrated HSK preparation is part of our student support package.

## 3. The CSC scholarship is real — but competitive

The Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) can cover full tuition, accommodation, and a monthly stipend. It is merit-based and competitive: students with a CGPA above roughly 3.5 and strong extracurriculars have the best chances. University-level scholarships are a parallel — often less competitive — route, and mostly partial.

## 4. The JW202 process takes time

The JW202 is the official visa application form for study, issued by the university together with your Admission Notice. From university offer to visa in hand can take 8–12 weeks. Apply early. Missing intake windows is one of the most common — and most avoidable — mistakes we see.

## 5. On-ground support is not a luxury

Airport pickups, dormitory registration, SIM cards, bank accounts — these operational details can overwhelm students who arrive alone. Our team provides structured first-week support in every city where we place students.

### Quick checklist before you apply

- Verify university recognition in your home country
- Check application deadlines (January–April for the September intake)
- Prepare academic transcripts and passport copies
- Secure proof of financial means (even for scholarship applicants)
- Register for HSK preparation early
