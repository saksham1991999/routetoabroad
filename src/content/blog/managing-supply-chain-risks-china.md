Supply chain disruptions cost businesses billions every year. For India–China trade specifically, the risks are amplified by distance, regulatory complexity, language barriers, and the sheer scale of China's manufacturing ecosystem. Based on the shipments we manage, here are the five risks we see most often — and the frameworks that counter them.

## Risk 1: Quality deviation after sample approval

The sample is perfect. The bulk shipment is not. This is the single most common complaint in China sourcing, and the root cause is almost always the absence of mid-production inspection. We mandate AQL-standard inspections at roughly 20% and 80% of production for every client — problems caught at 20% are corrections; problems caught at arrival are losses.

## Risk 2: Supplier financial instability

Factories can fold quickly, especially smaller operations. A 100% advance payment to a factory that then halts before shipping is the nightmare scenario. Standard mitigation: 30% deposit, 70% against copy of Bill of Lading, escrow for first engagements.

## Risk 3: Customs classification errors

A wrong HS code can mean goods held at customs, clearance delays, and reassessed duties. Electronics and machinery are particularly treacherous because category boundaries are technical. Our customs team pre-classifies every product and prepares complete document sets before goods reach port.

## Risk 4: Shipping delays and port congestion

Cyclical congestion at Guangzhou and Shanghai can add 7–15 days. Plan buffer stock, quote realistic lead times to your customers, and let someone watch port conditions daily — we re-route clients when congestion data says it pays.

## Risk 5: Communication breakdown

Language barriers, time zones, and ambiguous specifications are a combustible mix. Critical specifications — tolerances, materials, colours, packaging — must be documented in both English and Chinese, with photographs. One misunderstood email can produce an entire wrong production run.

### Our standard risk-mitigation checklist

- Factory audit before commitment
- Written specifications in English + Chinese with photo references
- Staged payment structure (30/70)
- AQL inspection at production milestones
- Port congestion monitoring and routing alternatives
- Customs documentation pre-prepared

Supply chain risk is manageable — but it requires systems, not just experience. If you have been burned by a previous supplier, our team can help you rebuild the procurement process properly.
